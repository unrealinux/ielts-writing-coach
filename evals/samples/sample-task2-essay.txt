Nowadays, with the development of society and economy, more and more people pay attention on the university education. Some people think that university education should be free, but other people think students should pay it by themselves. This essay will discuss both sides.

On the one hand, free university education has many advantages. Firstly, it can help the poor students to get the knowledge. Because education is very important for everyone. If the government pay the tuition, students from poor family can also go to university. Secondly, it is good for the society, it can make the country develop faster. Moreover, free education can reduce the gap between the rich and the poor.

On the other hand, students should pay for their own tuition. Firstly, the government has many things to do, such as build roads and hospitals. If all the education is free, the government will have a lot of pressure. Secondly, if students pay the money by themselves, they will study more hard, because they don't want to waste their money. Moreover, the university can use this money to improve the teaching quality.

In my opinion, I think the university education should not be completely free. Although free education is good, but the government cannot afford it. However, the government should provide some help for the poor students, for example, they can give them some scholarship and let them do some part-time job.

In conclusion, this is a complex issue and every coin has two sides. Both views have their own reasons. We should consider it carefully.
